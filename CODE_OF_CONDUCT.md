# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
doxxing, sexualized conduct, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable in
issues, pull requests, discussions, or other project spaces.

Project maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions and may temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when conduct harms people or the project.
For a sensitive conduct concern, open an issue asking the maintainer for a
private contact channel without posting personal details, or use GitHub's
Report Abuse process. Do not include another person's private information in a
public report.

Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact, repeated behavior, and the
safety of affected participants.
